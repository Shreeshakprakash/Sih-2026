GIS visual verification notes — 2026-09-04
The new Leaflet route renders with a dark intelligence-style map surface, custom thermal anomaly markers, layer panel, search control, dark/satellite toggle, zoom/current-location controls, and responsive mobile composition. External tile imagery was delayed in the preview renderer, so a self-contained SVG vector fallback was added behind the Leaflet layers; the map now retains geographic structure and grid context without depending on third-party tile timing. The final pass also defaults the first high-confidence detection open and makes roads, vegetation, water, facilities, historical events, anomalies, and heatmap toggles functional.
